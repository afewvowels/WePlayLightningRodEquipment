# WePlay: Lightning Rod Equipment

Personal equipment that attracts lightning on Fulgora and charges the batteries in your equipment grid.

## What it does

Turn yourself into a walking lightning rod. While you wear **Lightning Rod Equipment** on **Fulgora**, nearby lightning is pulled toward you, and the captured energy goes straight into the battery equipment in your armor's grid.

- **Personal lightning attractor:** strikes are drawn to you while the equipment is in your grid.
- **Charges your batteries:** captured energy fills your grid's battery equipment, including modded batteries.
- **Fulgora only:** the attractor is removed when you leave the planet, take the equipment off, or die.
- **Competes with base attractors:** the default attraction range is 64 tiles, so while you wear it, it pulls strikes away from nearby lightning rods and collectors. Lower the range in the startup settings if you would rather share.

## Requirements

- Factorio 2.1
- Space Age

## Installation

Install it from the in-game mod manager or from the mod portal page:

**[WePlay: Lightning Rod Equipment on the Factorio mod portal](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/WePlayLightningRodEquipment)**

To install from source instead, copy this repository's folder into your Factorio `mods` directory, named `WePlayLightningRodEquipment`.

## Unlocking

The equipment becomes craftable when you discover Fulgora.

## Settings

Startup settings let you tune lightning capture efficiency, attraction range (default 64 tiles) and the capture buffer size. Changing them requires a restart.
